I am self employed, how can I claim my boat?

Posted by: anonymous • Asked 4 hours ago

Hey everyone,

So I'm self-employed (consulting and some freelance stuff), and I bought a small boat last year that I sometimes use to meet clients or just unwind after projects. I'm wondering if there's any way I can claim part of the cost on my taxes — like as a business expense?

I’ve heard of people writing off all kinds of stuff, but I don’t want to do anything shady.
Any accountants here know if there's a legit way to do this? Like partial claim for “client entertainment” or something?

Joe Mama Responded 30 minutes ago

I actually went through something similar a couple years back. If your boat is used to generate income — like transporting clients, hosting meetings, or anything that directly supports your business — then some portion of the expenses could be deductible.

That said, the government is super strict about this kind of stuff, especially when it involves “fun” assets like boats or cars. You’ll need to document everything: when you used it, for what, who was with you, etc.

If you only use it occasionally for business, it’s probably safer to just expense those individual trips instead of claiming ownership costs. And definitely talk to a tax pro — the rules can be a bit murky depending on your country/province.

Hope that helps a bit!
